- Product Expansion uses Pluto as a product benchmark. It does not replace the governed runtime architecture.
- Capsule personal-memory portability is reviewed and scoped. Behavioral/Skill/Role/Pack activation remains inert pending destination qualification.
- Public managed provisioning, broader hosting, new signature protocols and incomplete live-channel campaigns retain source and explicit deferred status.
